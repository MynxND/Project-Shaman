# Thai shaman (หมอผี / อาจารย์ไสยเวท) VFX language — proposal 2026-10-10

User review of batch-1 seeds: "หลุดตีมหมอผีไทยไปเยอะ … แนวญี่ปุ่น ไต้หวันมากกว่า". The batch looked like East-Asian
xianxia/anime magic: neon energy columns, glowing crystal shields, lightning arcs, clean magic circles.
Thai shaman magic is **physical, ritual, folk and dirty**. Its power shows through objects and offerings, not pure light.

## 1. Use (Thai ritual materials as the effect itself)

| Motif | Thai | Use in effects |
|---|---|---|
| Sacred cotton thread | สายสิญจน์ | binding, wards, circles drawn by thread, snapping threads |
| Yantra cloth | ผ้ายันต์ (red/white cloth with grid-and-glyph patterns) | fluttering strips, burning cloth, shields of cloth |
| Black candles, incense | เทียนดำ, ธูป | ring of candles stuck in the ground, thick incense smoke, flickering amber flames |
| Holy water | น้ำมนต์, ขันน้ำมนต์ | sprayed droplets for purification |
| Prai oil | น้ำมันพราย | black oil puddles, oily drops that ignite |
| Rice and salt | ข้าวสาร, เกลือ | grains thrown to scatter spirits |
| Curse objects | ตะปู, เข็ม, หนังควาย, เส้นผม | nails, needles, hide pieces and hair ejected when a curse is broken |
| Amulets | ตะกรุด, ผ้ายันต์, ปลัดขิก (avoid) | small rolled metal amulets glinting |
| Offerings | บายศรี, ดอกไม้, หมาก-พลู | banana-leaf cone offerings around big rituals |
| Clay / straw puppet | หุ่นพยนต์ (ดิน, หญ้าแฝก) | clay lumps, straw, grass binding |
| Ghosts | ผีพราย (woman, long hair, Thai dress: สไบ, ผ้าซิ่น), ผีตายโหง | translucent, smoky, grasping hands with long nails |
| Ash, smoke | ขี้เถ้า, ควันธูป | falling ash, grey-violet smoke |

## 2. Avoid (reads Japanese / Chinese / anime)

Neon energy pillars, crystal or glass shields, lightning bolts, clean glowing magic circles with rotating rings,
ofuda paper talismans hanging vertically, jiangshi/kimono ghosts, sakura, clean geometric light beams, white silhouette
"holes", sparkle stars.

## 3. Colour and light

- Base: night darkness, **candle amber** light, **smoke grey-violet**, **old crimson** cloth (Thai theme red + purple kept
  as cloth/smoke/flame tints, not neon), bone-white thread, black oil, dull gold leaf (ทองคำเปลว).
- Light comes from candle flames and embers, never from neon energy.
- Purple appears as tinted smoke and spirit flame, desaturated.

## 4. User direction (2026-10-10, approved)

- **Yantra script and Khom-style ornament** (อักขระยันต์, ลายขอม): yantra lines, artistic glyph strokes and geometric
  grids based on Thai yantra instead of Western magic circles. Drawn as original ornamental Khom-style shapes, never a
  real readable mantra (bible: no copied sacred writing).
- **Yantra cloth and amulets** flutter, fold, unfold and burn instead of generic magic particles.
- **Hun phayon and ritual clay**: cracked earth, sacred thread, amulet fragments and sculpted figures are skill parts.
- **Thai folk ghosts and spirits**: นางพราย, and also กระสือ (floating head with a glowing trail; no visible organs),
  กระหัง (winnowing-basket wings), เปรต (very tall thin hungry ghost) where a skill calls for a horde.
- Skills carry **Thai names** next to the English ids.
