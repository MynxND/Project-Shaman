# Indonesia (Santet / Keris) VFX language — proposal 2026-10-10

Lesson from Thailand: each nation needs its own folk-occult grammar first, or effects drift into generic East-Asian
anime magic. Indonesia's black magic (ilmu hitam, santet, dukun, klenik) is likewise **physical, ritual and folk**.

## 1. Use

| Motif | Use in effects |
|---|---|
| Santet objects: needles, nails, glass shards (jarum, paku, beling) | the curse itself — flying into or bursting out of the victim |
| Kemenyan (benzoin) incense | thick grey-white smoke, the colour of every spell |
| Kembang tujuh rupa (jasmine/melati, kantil, rose petals) | petals falling, wilting and turning black |
| Sesajen offerings (banana-leaf baskets) | around big rituals |
| Black cloth, rajah talisman cloth | wrappings and strips with original ornamental Javanese-style glyph strokes (not real script) |
| Cloth dolls (boneka) | voodoo-like links and curses |
| Keris | wavy (luk) blade with pamor damascus pattern, wrangka sheath; poison and gold glints |
| Batik (parang, kawung) | ornamental borders and wave crests, never as a flat background |
| Wayang kulit | leather shadow-puppet silhouettes for shadows, stealth and clones |
| Folk spirits | kuntilanak (long-haired woman in white), pocong (shroud-bound), leak (Balinese flying head with a glowing trail, no organs), genderuwo (huge dark hairy hand) |

## 2. Avoid

Thai motifs (sacred thread, Khom yantra, sabai), Japanese/Chinese/anime magic (neon beams, crystals, lightning, clean
magic circles, ofuda), skulls, gore, readable real script, sacred religious symbols.

## 3. Colour (approved 2026-10-10)

Thailand = crimson + purple. Proposal for Indonesia: **black and indigo + antique gold (keris, batik) + poison green**,
with bone-white kemenyan smoke and shrouds. Santet leans black/indigo/gold; Keris leans black/poison green/gold.
