# National landmark requirement / user2026-10-15

Approved requirement: at least one map per country should be immediately recognizable through a distinctive regional landmark, translated into original shaman fantasy. Support with terrain, architecture silhouette and vegetation, not name labels/flags or a generic recolor. No copied sacred writing, real-site replica or third-party sprites. National architecture is a visual reference, not approval of geography, religion or new lore.

Current implementation scope: Indonesian 3 previews. ember-caldera composes an original large split-stone gateway before its stepped sanctum, with lake/volcanic landscape. Architectural orientation reference (text only, no image downloaded): Indonesia Ministry of Tourism, https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/travel-ideas/pilgrimage/pura-lempuyang . Original owned atlas reused, zero generations.

Other nations require their own reviewed landmark before art generation. These proposals do not mean their maps or iconic art are newly completed.

Natural boundaries: author broad coves, peninsulas, variable-width river bends and rounded path transitions; no random jitter substituted for geography.
