## Visible Semantic Envelopes

These are alpha-scanned within explicitly selected rectangles. They include
neighboring skin at boundaries and are not isolated anatomical part masks.
Width/height differences cannot be claimed as exact skeletal or tissue lengths.
Eye envelopes include lids/skin; V2's wider measurement window does NOT establish
larger eyeballs. New eyes are explicitly drawn8x7 lid envelopes,4x5 iris clusters,

## Actual Art Review

The native and individual4x PNGs were viewed, including the final shared face.
**A is the strongest response to the USER's compact/wider/softer brief:** only3px
shorter than V2 overall, but broader scalp/face and shoulders, shorter exposed legs
and no chest/abs divisions make a different silhouette. B retains more adult
vertical room; C shows why height-only ratios do not guarantee a compact read.
Remaining concerns: broad flat torso plane, angular flank/forearm clusters,
simplified mitten hands and wide toe silhouettes. No visual approval is inferred.
