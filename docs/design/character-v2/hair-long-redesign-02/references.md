# Visual Study - 2026-10-10

These official images were opened and visually inspected in the integrated browser.
Viewing only: no downloaded third-party assets, copied pixels, model input, training
data or sprite extraction. All new geometry is independently authored in art.json.

1. Ragnarok Origin Global, official Swordsman illustration:
   https://roglobal.com/ and
   https://roglobal.com/assets/imgs/p4_male_big-REVMph9N.png
   Observed: blond short cut with a pronounced side part, swept long fringe on one
   cheek, shorter opposite temple, open eyes. Used only for asymmetry and readable
   face framing, NOT as a long-hair example or sprite-generation reference.
2. Tree of Savior, official male Onmyoji class image:
   https://treeofsavior.com/page/class/view.php?c=Onmyoji
   Observed: blond long side fringe, curved parallel highlights following the
   crown, tapered fall beside the shoulder. The displayed small image obscures one
   eye; this draft deliberately keeps BOTH eyes visible instead.
3. Tree of Savior, official male Blossom Blader class image:
   https://treeofsavior.com/page/class/view.php?c=BlossomBlader and
   https://treeofsavior.com/img/class2/class_character/blossomblader_m.gif
   Observed: dark medium-length center/side sweep, grouped curved cheek locks,
   small outward flick at one temple and open central face. Used for grouping and
   curvature, NOT as evidence of waist-length hair.

Two attempted FFXIV Lodestone special-page URLs returned 404. They were NOT
viewed as character references and supply no design findings.

## Independent Design Principles

- Lifted asymmetric crown and swept curtain fringe rather than a row of spikes.
- Broad connected silver highlight planes that follow the curve; dark undersides,
  no scattered texture or copied strands.
- Five authored rear lock groups, with a few curved, separated waist tips instead
  of a solid straight cape or repeated triangular teeth. Body naturally occludes
  most rear hair in FRONT. No bow, red tie, accessory, or face redraw.

Limits: these are small official browser images, not high-resolution hair sheets.
The new pixel draft still needs USER art approval; references do not certify beauty.
