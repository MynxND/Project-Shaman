## Shape before surface

Use a one-color hair preview on the original bald base before shading. A short
cut rises about3–5 native pixels above the scalp; medium hair adds width mostly
behind the ears, and tied hair has a compact explicit knot exception. These are
starting targets, not automatic acceptance thresholds. Avoid uniform circular
caps, square ceilings and a single horizontal fringe bar. Parting and a small
number of irregular lock tips give the style identity. Roots meet the scalp
without filling every scalp pixel. Skin in an intentional part is legitimate.

## Five-color material

Each first candidate uses five original colors: dark outline, recess shadow,
base mass, midtone and small highlight. A uses muted plum-charcoal, B warm dark
brown, C green-charcoal. No white ramp, skin colors, metallic glints or copied RO
palette. Upper-left light matches the body. Broad dark-to-mid clusters establish
volume first; bright clusters occupy only a small portion of the mass. No
checkerboard dithering, random one-pixel speckles, semitransparent edge blur or
strand-by-strand noise. Purposeful single-pixel contour steps may remain.

Use a nominal1px selective dark edge at native scale. Do not outline every
internal lock in black; reserve recesses for changes of mass. Clusters should
flow along a lock, not form repeated horizontal stripes or glossy white patches.
Review at1x for readability and4x nearest for cleanup, never smooth resizing.

## Layer contract

Render hairBack / immutable base / hairMain / hairFringe / optional tie accessory.
Only publish necessary layers. A: main+fringe. B: back+main+fringe. C: one main
mass including knot; no accessory needed. HairBack is a FRONT-view depth layer,
not a generated BACK direction. All PNGs share128×128 and head origin. Skin is
never baked into hair. Integer editor offsets are experiments, not approved rig
changes. Hat rules must hide/replace a colliding knot rather than squeeze hair
or alter the skull. Actual equipment compatibility remains unverified.
