for either body presentation; no copied Ragnarok style IDs. Volumes are native
pixel targets relative to the current measured V2 scalp, subject to visual review.

A/B/C are the selected three tests from the USER brief. C/G/H/K/N borrow broad
ideas of gathering, braiding and tying hair; they do not claim authentic historical
Thai ritual status, a nation/branch assignment, or a copied ceremonial object.
Directional part, braid attachment and knot location must remain consistent in
future views, which are explicitly outside this pass.
