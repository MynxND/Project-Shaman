# Ragnarok reference study — 2026-10-10

Technique study only. No RO image is downloaded into the repository, extracted,
traced, recolored, embedded in the Inspector, or submitted to PixelLab. The USER
sheet has unknown provenance; it establishes taste, not an asset license or an
officially verified style numbering. Observations below are our visual analysis,
not claims about Gravity's internal production methods or numerical budgets.

Gallery visual observations: male examples include short broken crops, rounded
side parts, compact spikes, longer curtains and swept-back masses. Female
examples include straight fringe with rear sheets, long divided masses and
parted face framing. These are descriptive categories, not gender restrictions
for Shaman. Crown bulk follows the skull; longer length mostly extends behind
the face rather than enlarging the whole head. Fringe openings let skin define
the part. Dark recesses separate locks, while a few brighter groups describe
the crown turn. Front/rear examples retain a recognizable part and length.
The rotating examples and USER sheet suggest checking directional continuity,
but no directional sprites are produced in this task. Equipment fit remains a
per-style visual check, not a universal guarantee.

The gallery images are small and some animated. Exact source pixel counts,
palette sizes, outline widths and volume percentages were not measured. All
numeric limits in the Shaman guide are original proposed art constraints.

Our previous failures: excessive ivory coverage read as wigs; painting every
scalp annotation pixel made gray forehead bands; front rods read as wires;
geometry tests could pass despite those failures. Hair V3 starts with original
dark masses, head-specific roots and silhouette-first review.
