## Code contract

- `packages/shared/src/character.ts`: `CharacterAppearance` (`bodyType`, `skinTone`, `hairStyle`, `face`, `outfit`), 8 `FACINGS`, `facingFromVector()` for movement, skin ramps and `recolorSkin()` for future layered frames. Test: `pnpm --filter @shaman/shared test`.
- `apps/client/src/game/characters/CharacterRig.ts`: `preloadCharacterRig`, `CharacterSprite` (`setAppearance`, `setFacing`), anchored at the feet. `FoundationScene` shows the default male; `/?rig=preview` shows both characters in all 8 directions.
- Walk (2026-10-08, after the PixelLab Tier 1 upgrade): all 8 directions for both bodies are generated, no mirroring (mirroring flipped one-sided feathers and ribbons when turning diagonally). Male: skeleton-v3 group 891ac0d0-4292-4648-a16f-221b75b1af62; female: skeleton-v3 group 91a6f385-157c-4445-8cf5-ecc4a37ba001. South (towards the camera) uses a v3 custom walk started from a straight-on front frame (male group c4a08c4a-96d9-4e3b-b8f6-8f1ea62fab3b, female 5cc2fd28-94be-4760-8be5-35c34d9ffa2e).
- Front view: the idle south rotations were edited with `edit_image` into a straight-on symmetrical front view (user request); originals and prompts are in `docs/design/pixellab-main/front/`. Female used the second edit (seed 11) because the first lost the apron colours.
- Second pass (user: side walks too stiff, south looked choppy): south, east and west for both bodies were regenerated as v3 custom 12-frame loops with the idle rotation as both start and end frame (seamless loop) and an action description asking for knee bend, opposite arm swing, body bob and cloth/hair sway. Requests, prompts and group ids: `docs/design/pixellab-main/walk-v3custom/`. Diagonals and north stay skeleton-v3 (8 frames). At WALK_CYCLE_UNITS = 2 the 12-frame loops play at 24 fps and the 8-frame ones at 16 fps, both covering 2 units per cycle.
- Third pass (user: male walking south still leaned left): the male south loop turned its torso to three-quarter mid-cycle. Regenerated as v3 with an action description that locks torso, shoulders, hips and face square to the camera (group 32f80e06-b018-42bd-bfac-64a5c85af273); a pixminimax candidate (group f3b16dbd-df45-4df3-b657-5e795a7d892a) also stayed frontal but stepped less and lost colour, so v3 was kept. Female south was checked and stays frontal.
- Fourth pass (user: male idle still stood at an angle; wanted a straight front stance like a supplied GIF, used as a written pose description only, not as model input): `edit_image` kept the old stance, so a 6-frame v3 "settle into a straight symmetrical stance" animation was generated (group d98e6214-dae8-4b31-849a-c80886d492c2) and its last frame, cropped to 128x128, became the male south idle (`docs/design/pixellab-main/front/male-idle-straight.png`). The south walk was then regenerated from it with start = end = the new idle (group c66804c1-ca86-40cd-b60b-26f0b6272d5d). The settle pose lost the red hair ribbon and reads slightly lighter than other rotations.
- Fifth pass (walk-cycle spec from the user, 2026-10-08), measured against the user's reference GIF (8 frames at 100 ms; used only for motion measurement, never as model input or stored in the repo): the reference keeps head and torso within 0.1% of figure height horizontally; ours drifted 3-5% (whole figure shifting in the canvas). `tools/sprites/stabilize_walk.py` now shifts each raw frame by whole pixels so the upper-body centroid sits at the cycle median (0.5-0.8% after, i.e. under 1 px), leaving vertical bob and foot contact untouched. Raw PixelLab frames live in `docs/design/pixellab-main/raw/<body>/walk/`; the client gets the stabilised copies. WALK_CYCLE_UNITS became 8/3: 0.67 s per cycle, 8-frame cycles at 12 fps and 12-frame loops at 18 fps (spec: 8-12 fps for 8 frames; reference cycle 0.8 s). Animated preview at in-game timing: `docs/design/pixellab-main/walk-stabilized-preview.gif` (`tools/sprites/walk_preview_gif.py`).
- In-game test (dev build, 145 Hz, `window.__shamanDebug` dev-only read-out): forward, backward, left, right, diagonal and direction switches play the matching direction at 17.5 / 11.7 fps, facing never flickers, idle shows within 7 ms of release. It exposed a real slide-back after stopping (30-40 px): the server stepped a fixed 1/30 s per tick while Windows timers fired less often (server ran at ~75% speed), and its 20 ms rate limit could drop the stop message. Both fixed in GameRoom (real elapsed time, stops never rate-limited); post-stop drift is now 0.2-3.8 px.
- Sixth pass (user: the earlier straight preview walked straight, why does the game lean?): that preview showed only frames 0, 3, 6 and 9 of the 12-frame v3 loop; frames 4-8 turned the torso so the back feather tail swung into view. `tools/sprites/frontal_score.py` now scores each frame's upper-body left-right symmetry (straight idle 0.063, side view 0.254); v3 loops peaked at 0.11-0.12 even with stronger frontal-lock prompts (8-frame v3 group 70cd21a3-a3cd-49bc-ab73-73d09155a90f). Fix: a helper character created from the straight idle (`create_character` v3, id dbd5654d-8df6-4a4f-bcbb-d9d61bfbc220, so its south rotation is that idle) animated with skeleton-v3 `walking-8-frames` (group 9f198794-6b21-46a9-8b15-33df56cf8609): template skeleton, no torso rotation, every frame 0.05-0.08. It is the male south walk now (8 frames, 12 fps). Skeleton-v3 needs a few minutes after character creation before the 3D skeleton is ready. Review every frame (or score them) before accepting a cycle; never judge from a frame sample.
- Seventh pass (user: body straight but the face looked turned): the straight idle had a three-quarter head (hair swept to one side, open mouth). `edit_image` (seed 41, `docs/design/pixellab-main/front/male-face-*`) turned only the head to face the camera; head asymmetry 0.063 -> 0.050, body unchanged. It is the male south idle now, and a new helper character from it (id cf7d9971-d700-4862-b1d3-863137dca224) gave the skeleton-v3 south walk (group eebaa32c-2413-4c9c-8e73-31709fb34cfd): head asymmetry 0.04-0.05 in every frame (was 0.06-0.08), body worst 0.064.
- Idle (2026-10-08, user: characters should move while standing): PixelLab `breathing-idle` skeleton-v3, 4 frames at 5 fps (groups in `docs/design/pixellab-main/idle/groups.json`; male south from the front helper character so it matches the straight idle). Raw frames are archived and stabilised like the walk. The client shows the static frame for 0.6 s after stopping, then the breathing loop. Male north is excluded (the loop turned the body: 0.104 vs 0.064 frontal score) and stays static until regenerated. Female idle covers all 8 directions (south from a front helper character 83ea8a66-caff-4872-aeae-350e50c765f9; south 0.056-0.064 and north 0.042-0.049 frontal scores, close to the static frames).
- Frame canvases vary (128x164 up to 176x176), so the manifest stores `frameWidth`, `frameHeight` and an anchor per direction (`layouts`): x aligns the opaque-pixel centroid of frame 0 with the idle rotation, y is the median lowest-foot row across the cycle plus the idle frame's gap to the body anchor.
- Walk timing follows Ragnarok-style feet sync: fps = frames x WALK_SPEED / WALK_CYCLE_UNITS (8 x 4 / 2 = 16 fps); one cycle covers 2 world units. `FacingTracker` (packages/shared/src/facing.ts) holds a new facing for 70 ms before showing it and keeps the walked facing on stop, so pressing or releasing two keys a few ms apart no longer flashes a cardinal frame. Old template-mode walk frames were replaced.
- Hair, face, skin tone and outfit are baked into main-v1 frames (`'main-v1'` / `'apprentice-v1'`); `skinTone` is not applied yet.

## Customisation (planned)

Players will change hairstyle, face, skin tone and outfit. That needs per-direction layers aligned on one base body: a base body sprite, then hair/face/outfit layers. Options to evaluate with PixelLab: `create_character_state` / `edit_image` on the main character for variants, `transfer_outfit` for re-dressing animation frames. Skin recolouring needs a skin mask per frame.

## Rejected: base-v1 (ComfyUI)

A ComfyUI-inpainted grey-bodysuit base (seed 512) was briefly in the game and was rejected by the user as off theme on 2026-10-08. Its sources remain in `docs/design/comfyui/base-v1/` for the record; the runtime assets and builder were removed.

- Eighth pass (2026-10-09, user: the walk away from the camera did not look right; there was no basic attack; casting looked choppy). All jobs ran through `tools/sprites/animate_rig.ps1`: PixelLab `animate_character` v3, one direction per job, start and end frame = the direction's runtime idle rotation. South uses the front helper characters. Request logs: `docs/design/pixellab-main/{walk,attack,cast}/<body>-<direction>.json`.
  - **North walk:** new 12-frame loops.
    - Male: accepted first try, frontal score mean 0.066 (old 0.073).
    - Female: the first try turned sideways (0.14) and was rejected. The re-roll locks the back of the head and hair to the centre of the back: mean 0.045, worst 0.059.
    - Old frames are kept in `raw/<body>/walk-archive-2026-10-08/north`.
  - **Basic attack** (new, `attack`): an 8-frame spirit palm strike in all 8 directions, played once at 16 fps (0.5 s) by `CharacterSprite.playOnce`. The attacker turns to face the monster first. No weapon, since the rig has none.
    - Re-rolls: female south and male north (both turned sideways).
    - Female north-east: the first attempt is used minus one glitched frame (7 frames); the re-roll added slash streaks.
    - Rejected frames: `raw/<body>/attack-rejected/`.
  - **Cast v2:** 12 frames, up from 8.
    - The first prompt ("spell", "energy") made PixelLab paint rings and orbs around the hands in 7 female directions and the male south-east. Those were re-rolled as a purely gestural description.
    - `tools/sprites/rig_cleanup.py` removes leftover detached specks under 30 px at packaging time; raw frames are untouched.
    - Playback no longer stretches the frames across the cast time. `castPoseFrame` (shared) plays intro, then a ping-pong channel loop, then release frames at a steady 12 fps; the release gesture follows the server's release.
    - Rejected frames: `raw/<body>/cast-rejected/`.
  - **Cost:** about 167 generations (961 → 794).
  - **Evidence:** in-game captures in `docs/design/pixellab-main/screens-2026-10-09/`.
